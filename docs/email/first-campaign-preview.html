A better question opens a better conversation.

Not sure what to ask your audience first? Start with something specific:

What is one thing you wish we talked about more?

A focused question gives people a place to begin. Share your UnsaidBox link where your audience already spends time, then give them room to answer honestly. They do not need to create an account to respond.

Read the answers privately before deciding what to do next. When a sender has opted in to private follow-ups, you can ask for more context. Share only responses that are eligible for sharing; nothing is published automatically.
