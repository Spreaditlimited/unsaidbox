UnsaidBox: expanded keyword research

Google Ads > Tools > Keyword Planner > Discover new keywords > Start with keywords.
Run each batch below separately. These are research seeds, not validated article titles.
Use English, Google only, and one country at a time: United States, United Kingdom, Australia.
Remove default locations, including Nigeria, before selecting the intended country.
For direct comparison with the original exports, use September 2025 through August 2026.
Leave the optional website filter empty for this discovery pass.
Download all keyword ideas as CSV, not the campaign forecast. No ad campaign needs to be launched.
Keep average monthly searches, competition and bid columns; include monthly history if available.
Name files unsaidbox-ideas-[tools|prompts|education]-[us|uk|au].csv.
Three batches across three countries = nine exports. Start with tools-US if doing this in stages.
Other English-speaking countries can be researched later; do not combine locations initially.

Evaluation after export:
- Preserve country and date settings; blanks are not zero.
- Prioritize relevance to anonymous audience collection over raw volume.
- Do not sum close variants as distinct demand or interpret ad competition as SEO difficulty.
- Review search intent for each shortlisted cluster before choosing a blog vs product landing page.
- Merge overlapping topics; evaluate relationship keywords for audience/creator intent.
- Keep activation and safety articles even when they have no measured search volume, clearly labelled as such.
- Do not invent trend claims when monthly history is missing.
